# Video script: Scape Planner (2:53)

Timed to `devpost/scape-planner-demo-cut.mp4`. Each block has a voiceover
(about 150 words a minute, so it fits the block with a breath to spare) and a
short on-screen caption for a text-only version. Captions can also sit on
top of the voiceover. Keep music royalty-free, or leave it out.

## 0:00 to 0:24, the problem and the first build

**Voiceover**
> Aquascapers build a layout dry, before the water goes in, because moving a
> stone later clouds the tank for days. But judging scale with your hands
> full of wet soil is hard. Scape Planner puts the plan inside the tank, at
> real size, on SPECS. Here a sixty centimetre tank gets soil, stones and a
> lawn of hairgrass.

**Caption**
> Plan your aquascape at real size, inside the tank, before the water goes in.

---

## 0:24 to 0:55, the finished Iwagumi

**Voiceover**
> This is an Iwagumi: two tall stones leaning apart, smaller stones at their
> feet, a soil ridge rising to the back. The stones sit in the soil, and the
> carpet grows up to them. You walk around it like a real tank, and look
> down from above to check the balance. A coach card scores the layout
> against the rules of the style: focal point on a third, odd number of
> stones, the main stone's height and lean.

**Caption**
> Iwagumi, 60 cm. Stones sink into the soil, the carpet follows the hills,
> and the coach scores the style.

---

## 0:55 to 1:25, a 120 cm tank, hardscape

**Voiceover**
> Six standard tank sizes. A hundred and twenty centimetres now. White sand
> in front, soil banked at the back, grey stones, then tall root clusters
> in three groups. Every piece can be rotated, leaned, resized and
> mirrored, so a few models give a whole landscape.

**Caption**
> 120 cm. Sand, soil, stones and roots. Rotate, lean, resize and mirror any
> piece.

## 1:25 to 1:55, the hardscape before planting

**Voiceover**
> This is the moment aquascapers photograph from every angle before the
> plants go in. On glasses you don't need the photos: you stand where you
> will stand, and look. The thirds grid and depth zones are drawn on the

> sight.

**Caption**
> The hardscape, before planting. Seen from where you stand.

## 1:55 to 2:17, planting

**Voiceover**
> Then the plants. Moss is laid onto the wood. Anubias and Bucephalandra
> attach low on the roots and stones, and move with them. Crypts at the
> feet, and a wall of stem plants at the back.

**Caption**
> Moss and epiphytes grow on the wood. Stems fill the back.
