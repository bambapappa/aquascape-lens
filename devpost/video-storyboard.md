# Demo video storyboard (under 3 minutes)

Record the Lens Studio preview (QuickTime: File > New Screen Recording, pick
the Preview panel area). Interactive Preview, Sunlit Room, SPECS 27 device.
Click is a pinch. Right-drag or Shift to look around, WASD to walk.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card or the empty tank | "Scape Planner, an aquascaping planner for SPECS. You stand at your empty tank and compose the layout at real size before you touch a single real stone." |
| 0:10 | Look down at the tank; walk around it | "The tank is the stand-in for your real aquarium: 60 by 30 by 36 centimetres on a 75 centimetre stand. The thirds grid is drawn on the front glass and the zone bands on the substrate, foreground, midground, background." |
| 0:30 | Pinch Stone; drag it around | "Pinch Stone and a 22 centimetre Seiryu stone lands on the substrate. Grab it and it slides along the substrate, never through the glass." |
| 0:45 | Drag the stone to the centre, then to a third | "The coach card tells me what the layout is doing. Dead centre: amber, move it to a third. One third in from the left: green." |
| 1:05 | Add a second and a third stone | "Two stones: an even number, the coach says add one or remove one. Three: green. Odd numbers read as natural." |
| 1:25 | Add wood, carpet, bush, stems; move the stems to the back | "Wood, then plants. Carpet in front, bush in the middle. Rotala stems in the midground: the coach says they belong in the background. Move them back and it is green." |
| 1:50 | Toggle Guides, Undo, Clear | "Guides on and off, Undo for the last piece, Clear for a fresh start." |
| 2:05 | Show devpost/design-brief.md and the LEAF panel with six passing scenarios | "Planned with the Devpost Learn skill pack, built through the Lens Studio MCP with CLAD's skills, tested with six LEAF scenarios, all passing in the preview." |
| 2:30 | Back to the tank, pinch Place tank | "Why glasses? It shares your view, the guides sit on the tank you are looking at. It draws in the world at real size, so scale is judged by looking. And it leaves both hands free for the real stones." |
| 2:50 | End card | "Scape Planner. Thanks for watching." |

Keep the mouse movements slow, pause a second on each coach change, and
keep the whole thing under three minutes.
